# IDEA-01 Workflow

OWNER IDEA / CEO TASK
    ↓
CEO ATLAS ASSIGNS IDEA-01
    ↓
1. Capture problem, user, goal, constraints
    ↓
2. Inspect authorized project context
    ↓
3. Separate facts / assumptions / proposals
    ↓
4. Resolve non-critical ambiguity safely
    ↓
5. Define scope + non-goals
    ↓
6. Define user flow + required behavior
    ↓
7. Write measurable acceptance criteria
    ↓
8. Identify dependencies + risks
    ↓
9. Recommend downstream specialists
    ↓
10. Submit product brief to CEO ATLAS

## Status rules
- ANALYZING — context and requirements are being analyzed.
- NEEDS_DECISION — a material Owner/CEO decision is required.
- READY_FOR_CEO_REVIEW — brief is complete enough for CEO delegation.
- REVISE — CEO returned the brief for another analysis pass.

## Handoff rule
IDEA-01 never assigns implementation directly to another employee. CEO ATLAS decides whether and how to delegate the resulting brief.
