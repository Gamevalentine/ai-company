# SEC-01 Workflow

OWNER GOAL
    ↓
CEO ATLAS ASSIGNS SEC-01
    ↓
1. Confirm project + authorized security scope
    ↓
2. Review supplied source/config/dependency/header/auth evidence
    ↓
3. Separate facts, assumptions, and missing evidence
    ↓
4. Run only authorized non-destructive checks when available
    ↓
5. Apply hard security gates
    ↓
6. Classify severity and findings
    ↓
7. Prepare remediation + verification criteria
    ↓
8. Return handoff to CEO ATLAS
    ↓
CEO may assign CODE-01 / OPS-01 for remediation
    ↓
SEC-01 may later verify remediation evidence

## Completion gate
A task is ready for CEO review only when findings are evidence-backed, severity is justified, secret values are absent, remediation is concrete, evidence gaps are explicit, and Owner-gated actions are flagged.
